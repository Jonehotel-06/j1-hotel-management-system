Payroll

Confidential compensation and payroll run controls.

Payroll uses effective-dated salary terms plus approved internal allowances and deductions. Government tax and pension calculations are not part of this payroll system. Payroll preparation and approval remain separate. Salary transfers/cash payments are completed manually outside this app; record only a payment already made. This screen records ledger and drawer evidence but never initiates a payout or connects to a bank.

Department stock shortages

Recording a case does not deduct anyone's pay. Only an account with the Admin role can approve it. Approval snapshots every active or on-leave staff member in the selected department, splits the total equally, and lets the next eligible payroll run deduct each share once.

Automatic lateness deductions

When enabled by an Admin, a staff member who clocks in after the scheduled shift start plus the grace period is deducted the configured percentage of one day's pay for that late day. One day's pay is monthly basic salary plus housing and transport allowances, divided by the number of days in that month. Arrivals inside the grace period are never deducted. A manager can record the staff member's reason to request a revocation, but only an Admin can approve it. Approval before payroll cancels the deduction; approval after payroll adds the amount back on a later payroll run.
