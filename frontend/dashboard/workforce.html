Reception Biometric Attendance & Numeric Workstation Code Console

Scan staff fingerprint to clock in, start/end break, clock out (which immediately expires workstation access codes), or issue a 6-digit numeric workstation code. Attendance for operational staff is recorded only at an enrolled Receptionist Desktop — a browser carrying the desk key, or one at the workstation’s registered address. Management accounts (administrator, manager, general manager) that may authorise a supervised fallback can also record from elsewhere, and every such action is audited with the account and the reason. The server re-checks all of this; nothing here decides it.

Scan a staff member's fingerprint on the left to verify attendance status or issue a 6-digit numeric workstation access code.

Supervised fallback (manager / administrator)

Use only when the scanner or the employee's finger genuinely cannot be read. A manager fallback needs the recorded failure from the failed scan above; an administrator may authorise one without a prior attempt. This card works away from the front desk for management accounts — which is how a supervisor covers a reader outage on a non-reception device — and every fallback is audited with your account, this reason and the authority used. The issued code is single-use and expires when the employee clocks out. Ordinary staff can never issue one.

Select staff member (recommended)

Search the staff directory by name, account email or employee code, then select the exact person. This sends the account staff ID directly.

If this is blank, a staff member selected in the staff search—or the identifier in the biometric terminal field—will be used.
