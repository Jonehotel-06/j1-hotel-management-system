Laundry is priced per item at reception and processed through Received → In progress → Ready → Delivered. An in-house guest's order is billed once to their folio (Bill to folio); a walk-in customer pays at the desk (Record payment) and the money posts to the ledger immediately. Delivery asks who received the items. Walk-in orders are never charged to a folio, because there is no guest account to charge.
