Financial statements

Balance sheet and cash flow from the same posted ledger
How these statements are built

Balance sheet and cash flow are computed from the same posted ledger as every figure above — no separate statement books. Opening balances are posted as balanced journals against Equity · Opening balance offset (Post journal entry) dated before go-live. Equity includes current earnings (all posted revenue less expenses to date, not yet closed into retained earnings). Cash-flow lines are classified once from each transaction's counter-accounts: equity counters are financing, everything else in this chart is operating. Drawer float openings are internal and excluded.
