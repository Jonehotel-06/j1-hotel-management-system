Receptionist Desktop enrolment

A front desk PC can be enrolled two ways: install the issued desk key into this browser (below), or register this browser’s address for the front desk workstation (the registry row action). With a registered address, front desk staff sign in with their email and password alone — nothing to type. Operational staff cannot sign in anywhere else; management roles sign in anywhere as before.
