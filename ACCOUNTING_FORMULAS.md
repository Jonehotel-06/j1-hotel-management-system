# Accounting formulas (Batch 02)

All money filters use posted immutable `FinancialTransaction`/`FinancialLine` entries within inclusive `business_date` range and currency. The UI uses the exact decimal strings from the API; NGN display uses `JONE.formatNaira`. No customer-facing tax is introduced.

| Accounting overview | Source and formula |
|---|---|
| Recognized revenue | Sum credits minus debits for all `REVENUE.*` ledger lines. Includes `REVENUE.ACCOMMODATION` (nightly room charges), `REVENUE.FOOD_BEVERAGE` (POS, bar/restaurant/room service), `REVENUE.OTHER_OPERATING` (supported paid hotel services), `REVENUE.SERVICE_FEE` and any other posted `REVENUE.*`. Collections are **not** revenue a second time. Thus this card must **not** be renamed “Bar and Restaurants”. |
| Total Collections | `PAYMENT_COLLECTION` debits to `ASSET.CASH_ON_HAND`, `ASSET.BANK_CLEARING` and `ASSET.CARD_CLEARING`. Includes booking/POS/service payment collections where those flows post this journal type. Does not net refunds; net collections appears separately. |
| Operating expenses | Posted `EXPENSE` debits to `EXPENSE.OPERATING`, less credits to that account (reversals). Includes unpaid expenses as **accrual expenses**, not disbursements. |
| Net operating result | Recognized revenue − discounts (debits − credits to `CONTRA_REVENUE.*`) − operating expenses − accrued payroll expense (debits to `EXPENSE.PAYROLL`/pension expense less credits). API compatibility key: `gross_operating_result`; not a cash figure or full statutory net profit. Equal numeric values when expenses are zero are coincidental, not duplicate formulas. |
| Outstanding supplier payables | Posted `LIABILITY.ACCOUNTS_PAYABLE` opening credit balance before From, plus credits less debits in range. Balance as of To, not period cash flow. |
| Total cash disbursed | Sum posted `EXPENSE`/`PAYROLL_PAYMENT` credits to cash/bank/card, less posted debit tender reversals explicitly linked to one of those types. The summary block splits it into supplier/inventory payments, paid operating expenses, net salaries and a residual “other”; residual ensures parts sum **once** to the total. Refunds are deliberately separate, not supplier payouts. Expense invoices on account and unposted proposals do not credit a tender account and therefore do not enter this total. |

The cash disbursement residual may absorb reversals of supplier/operating payouts when their reversal has no settlement classification; total still ties exactly to posted tender movements. The selected period can have negative net movements after refunds of previously paid costs. `cash_paid_out` is **physical cash from EXPENSE only**; `actual_payments_made` is a legacy partial measure and is not the Total cash disbursed card. `net_cash_movement` = collections − guest refunds − total cash disbursed.

## Profit and loss / statements

Revenue by department is a signed grouping of the *same* `REVENUE.*` lines (never added again to revenue). Bar, restaurant and room service share the food/beverage account; separate item-type P&L categories cannot be derived from current chart codes without guessing. Laundry/other paid services appear in the existing service/other operating revenue account when posted there. The P&L renders accrued payroll and discounts explicitly. COGS is not fabricated from stock consumption (no posted COGS account). Historical tax liabilities remain in balance-sheet accounts if they have a posted balance; no new hotel statutory accrual is posted.

AR aging: booking stay balance (`amount_due`, entire multi-room booking snapshot) + net posted *non-accommodation* folio charge postings across **all** folios with that booking ID, excluding accommodation settlement postings already reflected in `amount_paid`; negative totals are clamped to zero for outstanding receivables, with positive charges included even on fully paid stays. This is an operational current-balance projection. The folio endpoint computes signed balances independently from its immutable postings and exposes room/stay references where available. The balance sheet uses signed ledger AR as-of the selected date; an operational booking projection should not be presented as the historical ledger balance.
